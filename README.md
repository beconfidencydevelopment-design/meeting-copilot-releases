# Meeting Copilot

A Mac app that listens to your client calls and suggests what to say, takes notes,
and writes the summary and follow up email when the call ends.

This repository only hosts the downloads.

## Install

1. [Download Meeting Copilot](https://github.com/beconfidencydevelopment-design/meeting-copilot-releases/releases/latest/download/Meeting-Copilot.dmg)
   (macOS 26 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel).
2. Open the DMG and drag Meeting Copilot onto Applications.
3. Open it from Applications. macOS says it can't check the app; click Done.
4. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.
5. Follow the welcome screen. You need your own Claude API key from platform.claude.com.

## Updates

Automatic. When there's a new version you get a notification; click it and Meeting Copilot
updates and reopens in a few seconds. Each update is checked against the Meeting Copilot
signature before it installs.

Your calls, notes and clients stay on your Mac.
